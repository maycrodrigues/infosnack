Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

In short: the InfoSnack app only accesses the channel's official accounts, keeps the minimum needed to publish videos, stores everything on the team's computer and never sells or shares data. This website uses no cookies or tracking.

1. Who we are

InfoSnack is a short-form video news channel. The channel's team is responsible for the data handled by the InfoSnack app (the “app”). Contact: maycrodriguess@gmail.com.

2. What data the app accesses

When a team member connects an official InfoSnack account to a social network, the app receives from that network:

  • Basic account profile: identifier, display name, username and profile picture.
  • Access credentials: the tokens the network issues so the app can publish on behalf of the account.
  • Publishing data: what the network provides for an upload to succeed, such as the allowed privacy options and maximum video length (TikTok) or the pages the account manages (Facebook).
  • Publishing results: the link and identifier of each post made by the app.

The app does not access messages, followers, likes, comments, viewer data or payment data.

3. How we use it

Only to publish the videos the team chooses and to show the result in the app. We do not use this data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

4. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Data is stored only on the team's computer, in files that only the operating-system user can read.
  • There is no InfoSnack server: data is never sent anywhere other than the networks themselves.
  • Communication with the networks uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Sharing

We do not share data with third parties. The only exceptions are the network that receives the post and cases where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

An account's profile and tokens are kept while the account is connected and deleted as soon as it is disconnected. The link to each post is kept with the video's project until the project is deleted.

7. YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the InfoSnack channel. By connecting a YouTube account, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. TikTok, Meta and other networks

On TikTok, the app uses Login Kit and the Content Posting API, with the user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish scopes. On Meta (Instagram, Facebook and Threads), X, LinkedIn and the other networks, the app only requests the permissions needed to publish and to read the basic profile. Each network also handles data under its own privacy policy.

9. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking tools and collects no data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical access data, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Your rights (LGPD)

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018), you may ask us to confirm whether we process your data, and to access, correct or delete it, or withdraw an authorization. Write to maycrodriguess@gmail.com and we will reply within 15 days. See also How to delete your data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the version in effect.
