The InfoSnack app

The InfoSnack app is the production and publishing tool used by the channel's team. It runs on the team's own computer and is not offered to the public.

Produces

Writes the script from news published by public sources, generates the narration and edits the video in landscape (16:9) and vertical (9:16).

Publishes

Uploads the finished video to InfoSnack's official profiles on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and other networks, through each network's official API.

No audience data

Only the channel's official accounts are connected. The app does not read messages, followers or any viewer data.
