How to delete your data

Effective October 19, 2026

The InfoSnack app only keeps the basic profile and access tokens of connected accounts, plus the links to the posts it made. Details in the Privacy Policy. You can delete this data in two ways.

1. Revoke access on the network

Removing InfoSnack from your account's connected apps cuts the app's access immediately, and its tokens stop working.

  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook: Settings → Apps and websites
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, open Settings and privacy and look for the connected apps permissions.
  • X: x.com/settings/connected_apps
  • LinkedIn: Permitted services
  • Other networks: in the connected apps or security area of your account settings.

2. Request deletion by email

Send an email to maycrodriguess@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data — InfoSnack”, stating the network and the account's username. We delete that account's profile, tokens and post records within 15 days and confirm by email.

What happens next

  • The account's profile, tokens and records are deleted from the team's computer.
  • Posts already published stay on the network. To remove them, delete them on the network itself.
